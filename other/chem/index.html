IMF

IMF stands for InterMolecular Forces. Each Molecule can have different forces.
There are different types of IMF. Here are a few: London Dispersion Forces, Dipole-Dipole Forces & Hydrogen Bonding.

3 Main IMF

London Dispersion Forces

London Dispersion forces happen in every molecule and is the result of electrons randomly moving more to one side of the atom than the other. These forces are relatively weak.

Dipole-Dipole Forces

Dipole-Dipole Forces happen when a molecule is polar because of its electron negativity.
Molecules can be polar when an atom with a low electronegativity is bonded with an electron with a high electronegativity (see the periodic table and the line of increasing electronegativity).

Hydrogen Bonding

Hydrogen bonding forces are the strongest intermolecular force and they happen when H is bonded to N,O or F
